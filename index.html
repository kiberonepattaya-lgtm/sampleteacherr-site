🎈 About Me

I'm a passionate coding instructor at KIBERone, teaching students in Pattaya about programming and technology. I love helping young minds discover the power of coding and bringing their creative ideas to life through technology. When I'm not teaching, you can find me enjoying entertainment or exploring new programming concepts.

🎊 My Hobbies

Watching series and movies is my favorite way to unwind after a long day of teaching. I enjoy everything from thrilling action films to thought-provoking dramas and documentaries. Beyond entertainment, I'm also deeply interested in coding projects, game development, and staying updated with the latest technology trends in education.

🎮 My Favorite Games

  • Minecraft — the perfect classroom sandbox: redstone circuits and command blocks teach real logic while it still feels like play.
  • Portal 2 — a physics puzzler that rewires how you think about problems, and the co-op campaign is unbeatable for teamwork.
  • Roblox — my students love it, and building their own games in it is often the moment coding finally clicks for them.
